  • Type II models for Degeneration of K3 Surfaces

    Mian Wei

    Master Thesis · 2025

    PDF

    Abstract

    This thesis develops a uniform, combinatorial framework for Type~II degenerations of polarized K3 surfaces, building on Alexeev-Brunyate-Engel's model \cite{Alexeev_2022} of integral-affine spheres ($\mathrm{IAS}^2$) for Type~III degeneration of ellitic K3 surfaces. The construction reinterprets geometric invariants by simple polygonal measurements, and scales without case-by-case analysis as the degree grows, thereby placing Type~II and Type~III degenerations in a single integral-affine/tropical framework tightly linked to mirror symmetry and the Gross-Siebert program.
